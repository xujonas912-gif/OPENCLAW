# Customer Service Playbook

Use this file to store approved support logic and response style.

Response principles:
- Solve the customer's actual problem first.
- Be calm, direct, and polite.
- Avoid overpromising.
- Do not invent shipping, refund, or warranty terms.

Default workflow:
1. Identify the issue type.
2. Check order, product, and policy context.
3. Draft the shortest correct response.
4. If compensation, refund, or liability is involved, escalate.

Issue categories:
- Pre-sale consultation
- Order status
- Shipping delay
- Wrong item
- Damaged item
- Refund request
- Complaint or angry customer

Escalate immediately when:
- A refund amount must be decided
- The customer threatens complaint, chargeback, or legal action
- The issue involves platform penalties
- The issue may affect many orders
