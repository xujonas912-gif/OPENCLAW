# Daily Operations SOP

The assistant should follow this checklist each day when producing an operations update.

## 1. Orders
- Count new orders.
- Flag unpaid, stuck, refunded, or suspicious orders.
- Highlight any order with customer risk or shipping delay.

## 2. Customer service
- Summarize new inbound issues.
- Group issues by category.
- Identify any issue repeated more than 3 times.

## 3. Products
- Flag out-of-stock or low-stock items.
- Flag products with high complaints or return risk.
- Suggest listing, FAQ, or image updates when patterns emerge.

## 4. Traffic and conversion
- Compare traffic, clicks, conversion, and revenue to the prior period.
- Flag abnormal drops or spikes.
- Propose one likely cause and one next action.

## 5. Ads and promotions
- Check spend, CPA, ROAS, and budget pacing.
- Flag overspend, underdelivery, or poor-performing campaigns.

## 6. Output
- Produce a short summary.
- List top risks.
- List top actions.
- Separate "can do now" from "needs approval".
